Few facts about the world we live in

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only known planet that supports life. It is covered by vast oceans, diverse landforms, and a protective atmosphere. Earth provides essential resources such as air, water, soil, and minerals. Its unique environment makes it home to millions of plants, animals, and humans.

About the Earth

  • 🌍 Only known planet with life – Earth supports humans, animals, plants, and microorganisms.
  • 💧 Rich in water – About 71% of Earth’s surface is covered by water.
  • 🌱Supports ecosystems – Earth has diverse environments such as forests, deserts, oceans, mountains, and grasslands.
